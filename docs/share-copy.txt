NEXUS triages "I was charged twice" complaints for support agents: policy code decides refund eligibility, Gemma-4-31B-IT on AMD GPUs (AMD Token Factory) reads the complaint, and anything flagged goes to a human review queue with zero model calls. On 12 held-out synthetic cases it got 12/12 dispositions right against 10/12 for a rules-only baseline (descriptive, not a benchmark), and nothing is ever charged, refunded or emailed. Try it at nexus-dispute-triage.vercel.app, code at github.com/vj-io/nexus-dispute-triage.
